# Code of Conduct

Housie Ka King welcomes people of all backgrounds, identities, abilities, and experience levels. This code applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other interactions in the repository.

## How we work together

- Be respectful and patient, especially when someone is new to the project.
- Give specific feedback about the work. Explain disagreements without personal attacks.
- Respect accessibility needs, privacy, and personal boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone’s private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

Use GitHub’s [reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) to flag an issue, pull request, comment, or account. Choose **Report to repository admins** if offered, or use **Report abuse to GitHub Support**, including when the concern involves a maintainer. Include a link and a brief description. Keep personal or sensitive details out of public issues.

Maintainers may remove or hide content, lock a conversation, or restrict participation based on the severity and pattern of the behavior. Maintainers should limit sharing of reports to people who need the information to address them.
